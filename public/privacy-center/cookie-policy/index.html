Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how JurisBranded uses cookies and similar technologies on jurisbranded.com, and how you can control them. It applies alongside our Privacy Policy.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to make the site work, to remember your preferences, and to understand how the site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, and tags, do comparable things. We group everything this site uses into the categories below.

The Categories We Use

Strictly necessary. These cookies are required for the site to function: page navigation and security. They are always on and cannot be switched off, because the site cannot work properly without them.

Statistics. These help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site. This site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not share it for advertising.

Marketing. This site does not run third-party advertising networks and sets no marketing cookies.

Your Consent and Controls

You can decline statistics cookies at any time through Opt-Out Preferences in our Privacy Center.

You can also control cookies through your browser, blocking or deleting them, or browsing privately. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track” signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of any non-essential cookies and of any “sale” or “sharing” of your information.

Cookies Set by Others

Some pages include content from third parties: on this site, the booking calendar at book.jurisbranded.com and any embedded videos. Those providers may set their own cookies when their content loads, governed by their own policies. We limit such embeds and disclose them where used.

Changes to This Policy

If we add or change the technologies this site uses, we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact Us

Questions about cookies can be sent to our privacy team (support@jurisbranded.com), or submitted through the Privacy Center.
