Opt-Out Preferences

JurisBranded respects your right to control your personal information. This page explains our position on selling and sharing, and gives you the controls to opt out.

We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

JurisBranded does not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not engage in these practices, there is nothing you need to do to be excluded from them. We state it plainly here so you know where we stand.

Manage Cookies on This Site

This site uses Google Analytics statistics cookies and no advertising cookies. Essential cookies are always required for the site to work. To switch off statistics cookies, block or delete cookies in your browser, use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on, or turn on Global Privacy Control. See our Cookie Policy for what each category does.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we honor it automatically as an opt-out of any non-essential cookies and of any sale or sharing of your information.

Ask Us to Act on Your Information

You can also send us a direct request. Through our Privacy Center you can:

  • ask us to delete the information we hold about you (“Forget Me”);
  • access or receive a copy of your information (“Request My Data”);
  • correct inaccurate information (“Correct My Data”); and
  • unsubscribe from any communications.

Prefer to email?

Send your request by email (support@jurisbranded.com) and our privacy team will action it.

No Discrimination

We will never deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a lesser experience because you exercised any of these choices. We use the details you provide only to verify and fulfill your request.
